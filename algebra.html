Lesson 1: Linear Equations

Explanation:

A linear equation is an equation where the highest power of the variable is 1. It usually looks like: ax + b = c.

Example:

2x + 3 = 7
Step 1: Subtract 3 → 2x = 4
Step 2: Divide by 2 → x = 2
